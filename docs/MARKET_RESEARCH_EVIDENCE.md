## ⚖️ 3. Legal Backing & Statutory Frameworks (Judge Defensibility)

Why debtors legally MUST take these calls and pay:

1. **India: Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (MSMED) Act, 2006:**
   * **Section 15:** Mandates buyer payment within agreed terms (maximum 45 days).
   * **Section 16:** Failure to pay attracts **compound interest at 3x the RBI bank rate** calculated monthly.
   * *RevClaim AI Leverage:* Agent respectfully reminds Indian corporate buyers of Section 16 interest accumulation if payment is delayed further.

2. **United States: Freelance Worker Protection Acts (2024–2025):**
   * **New York State Freelance Isn't Free Act** (Effective August 2024).
   * **California Freelance Worker Protection Act** (SB 988, Effective January 1, 2025).
   * *RevClaim AI Leverage:* Mandates 30-day payment timelines and awards **double damages** + attorney fees if a client defaults.

3. **United Kingdom & EU:**
   * *Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998* allows statutory interest of 8% plus the Bank of England base rate, plus fixed compensation fees per invoice.
